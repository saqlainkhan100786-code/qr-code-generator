What Is a QR Code?

A QR code is a two-dimensional code that can store information such as website links, text and other data. Smartphones and other devices can scan QR codes to quickly access the information they contain.

QR codes are commonly used to share websites, contact details, business information, event information and other digital content.

What Can You Use a QR Code For?

  • Sharing website URLs
  • Sharing text and information
  • Providing contact information
  • Sharing business information
  • Sharing event information
  • Providing quick access to online links
